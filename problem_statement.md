# 3. Problem Statement

## Context
The daily commute is a critical part of urban life, with metro trains serving as a primary mode of transport for millions of passengers. However, the traditional process of managing metro train travel often relies on manual ticketing systems, long queues at station counters, and disjointed information boards that do not provide real-time or centralized updates to the commuters. 

## The Problem
Passengers frequently face the following issues:
1. **Time Inefficiency:** Commuters waste significant amounts of time standing in line to purchase or cancel tickets.
2. **Lack of Accessibility:** Passengers cannot easily view train schedules or check their booking status remotely before arriving at the station.
3. **Data Management Limitations:** Without a digital profile, passengers have no easy way to track their travel history or manage multiple bookings simultaneously.

## The Solution (MET-RO)
There is a pressing need for a unified, digital reservation system that empowers passengers to manage their metro travel independently. 

The **MET-RO: Metro Train Reservation System** addresses these challenges by providing a centralized, interactive Command Line Interface (CLI) application. It allows users to securely register and log in, view available train schedules in real-time, book tickets instantly, and seamlessly cancel or review their active bookings. By digitizing the ticketing workflow, MET-RO aims to reduce station congestion, save commuters time, and provide a frictionless, user-friendly travel management experience.
